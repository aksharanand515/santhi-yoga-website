Daily walk-in classes

Step onto the mat, wherever your journey began

Traditional Hatha Yoga sessions for visitors, travellers and local practitioners in Fort Kochi.

Each class follows the traditional Hatha approach: mindful asana, conscious breathing and a closing stillness, taught at a pace that suits the students in the room. Come for one class or return every day of your stay; there is no long-term commitment.

  • Morning sessions
  • Evening sessions
  • All levels welcome
  • Traditional Hatha approach

Class times vary through the season, so message us for today’s schedule.

Private yoga

One-to-one practice, shaped around you

Personalised sessions focused on your body, your goals and your pace.

Private sessions give time to look closely at how you move and breathe, and to build a practice you can continue on your own. They suit beginners who want a careful start as well as experienced students refining their practice.

  • Alignment
  • Mobility
  • Strength
  • Breath
  • Individual practice development

Yoga retreats in Kerala

Slow down, the Kerala way

Immersive retreats combining yoga, meditation, cultural experiences and unhurried, restorative time.

Retreat days balance time on the mat with Kerala’s culture, landscapes and quiet. Each retreat is shaped by the same traditional approach taught at the school.

  • Yoga
  • Meditation
  • Pranayama
  • Traditional philosophy
  • Kerala culture
  • Nature
  • Rest
  • Connection

How to book

Three simple steps

There is no online booking system. You speak to the school directly, so every question is answered by a person.

  1. Send a message

    WhatsApp, call or email with what you would like to join and when you are in Fort Kochi.

  2. Confirm the details

    We reply with class times, private session availability or training dates.

  3. Come to Chirattapalam

    Arrive at the school in Fort Kochi and step onto the mat.

Questions about classes

Can’t find an answer? Contact us

Do I need yoga experience?

No. Daily classes are open to all levels, and private sessions are shaped around where you are starting from.

What time are the classes?

There are morning and evening sessions. Times vary through the season, so message us on WhatsApp for today’s schedule.

Can I join for just one class?

Yes. Walk-in classes have no long-term commitment, so travellers can join for a single class or return throughout their stay.
