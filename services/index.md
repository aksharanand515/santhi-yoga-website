## Questions about classes

Can’t find an answer? [Contact us](https://santhiyogaindia.com/contact/)

**Do I need yoga experience?**

No. Daily classes are open to all levels, and private sessions are shaped around where you are starting from. [Your first class](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/first-yoga-class-beginners-guide/)

**What time are the classes?**

Every day, mornings 7:30–9:30 am and afternoons 3:30–5:30 pm. Some mornings the class moves to Secret Garden, 8:00–9:30 am, so message us on WhatsApp the evening before a morning class.

**Can I join for just one class?**

Yes. Walk-in classes have no long-term commitment, so travellers can join for a single class or return throughout their stay.

**How long is each yoga class?**

Two hours. That is longer than most yoga classes, and deliberate: it leaves room for breathing practice before the postures and a proper twenty-minute relaxation at the end. [Inside a two-hour class](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/inside-a-two-hour-hatha-class/)

**Are there yoga classes for beginners in Fort Kochi?**

Yes — every daily class is open to complete beginners. Postures are offered in easier versions, the pace is steady rather than fast, and around a third of the two hours is spent breathing and resting. [How often to practise](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/how-often-should-you-do-yoga/)

**Is this Sivananda yoga?**

The classes follow the traditional order Master Aksharanand trained in within the Sivananda tradition: relaxation, pranayama, sun salutations, the classical postures and a long final relaxation. The school itself is independent and is not an official Sivananda Yoga Vedanta centre. [Sivananda yoga in Kochi](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/sivananda-yoga-kochi/)

**Where should I stay for the morning class?**

Anywhere in Fort Kochi itself is within walking distance of the 7:30 class; the lanes around Chirattapalam are closest. Ernakulam, across the harbour, is too far for a daily practice. [Where to stay in Fort Kochi](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/where-to-stay-in-fort-kochi/)

**How much does a class cost?**

₹500 a class (a limited-time offer; normally ₹600, and ₹700 from November 2026). There are no packages or memberships, but if you practise with us for more than a week, classes are 30% off. Private sessions are ₹1,500, rising to ₹2,000 in November 2026. [Yoga class fees in Kochi](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/yoga-class-fees-kochi/)

**Do I need to bring a mat?**

No, mats are provided. Wear clothes you can bend in, and bring water and a light layer for the final relaxation. [What to wear](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/what-to-wear-yoga-class-india/)

**Is there a meditation class?**

Yes. We run a separate meditation class, with timing that depends on the season and availability, so message us to ask when the next one is. [Meditation in Fort Kochi](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/meditation-fort-kochi/)
