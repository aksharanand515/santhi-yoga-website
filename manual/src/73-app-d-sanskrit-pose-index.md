Postures in this manual, alphabetically by Sanskrit name (ignoring diacritics), with English equivalents. Most Sanskrit posture names are compounds ending in *āsana*: *paścima* (west, the back of the body) + *uttāna* (intense stretch) + *āsana* gives *paścimottānāsana*, the “intense stretch of the back of the body”. Learning a few dozen common elements — *ardha* (half), *eka* (one), *pāda* (foot), *hasta* (hand), *supta* (reclining), *baddha* (bound), *parivṛtta* (revolved), *ūrdhva* (upward), *adho* (downward), *mukha* (face) — makes most names transparent.
