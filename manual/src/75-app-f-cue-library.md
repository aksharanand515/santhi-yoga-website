A working collection of cues for the practices in this manual, gathered from the chapters on the twelve postures, prāṇāyāma, relaxation and teaching skills. Use them as a starting point, not a script: the best cue is the one that helps the student in front of you, in your own voice (@ch:teaching-skills). Each entry gives the pose or moment, the cue, and why it works or what to watch for.
