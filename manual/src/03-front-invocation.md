<p style="font-family:'Noto Serif Devanagari';font-size:15pt;line-height:2;color:#5C3B24">ॐ सह नाववतु । सह नौ भुनक्तु ।<br>सह वीर्यं करवावहै ।<br>तेजस्वि नावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै ।<br>ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥</p>

<p style="font-family:Cormorant;font-style:italic;font-size:14pt;line-height:1.5;color:#3F4A2E;margin-top:8mm">oṃ saha nāv avatu · saha nau bhunaktu<br>saha vīryaṃ karavāvahai<br>tejasvi nāv adhītam astu · mā vidviṣāvahai<br>oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ</p>

<p style="font-size:10pt;line-height:1.6;color:#4A463A;margin:8mm 30mm 0">May it protect us both, teacher and student.<br>May it nourish us both.<br>May we work together with energy.<br>May our study be bright and effective.<br>May we not turn against each other.<br>Om — peace, peace, peace.</p>

<p style="font:500 7pt Inter;letter-spacing:.2em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#8A6A2C;margin-top:10mm">Peace invocation of the Taittirīya and Kaṭha Upaniṣads,<br>traditionally chanted at the beginning of study</p>
