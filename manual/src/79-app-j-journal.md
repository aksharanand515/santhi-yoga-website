A journal turns practice into learning. Spend five minutes each evening of the course completing one page; copy the template for the weeks after the course. Over time, the journal shows you patterns — what helps, what disturbs, how the mind and body change — that no teacher could show you. It is private: write honestly.

::: tip How to use the journal
Write briefly and specifically. “Headstand: 2 min, steadier; neck fine” is more useful than “good practice”. Note one thing you noticed about your mind, not only your body. Once a week, read back over the week and write a short summary.
:::
