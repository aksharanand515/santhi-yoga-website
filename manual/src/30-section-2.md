The body is the first instrument of Haṭha Yoga, and a teacher must know it both from the inside, through practice, and from the outside, through anatomy. This section builds a working knowledge of the skeleton, muscles, breath and nervous system — always connected to what happens on the mat — and then applies it to the twelve basic postures, the safety of students, and the practices of prāṇāyāma and meditation.
