Before yoga was a set of postures it was a way of understanding the mind and a path towards freedom. This section traces that path from the Vedic hymns and the Upaniṣads, through the Bhagavad Gītā and Patañjali’s Yoga Sūtras, to the Haṭha texts and the practice as it is taught today — and it introduces the inner practices, the subtle-body maps and the responsibilities that a teacher carries.
