Knowing yoga and teaching it are different skills. This section turns practitioners into teachers: the qualities and ethics of a good teacher, inclusive and trauma-aware teaching, the craft of cueing, demonstration and observation, sequencing and class planning, touch and consent, the management of a room, and the practical realities of a teaching life — ending with the practicum and the final teaching examination.
