# OpenSim model data

`Rajagopal2016.osim` and the bone meshes in `geometry/` come from the OpenSim model
repository (opensim-org/opensim-models) and are distributed under CC BY 3.0.

- Rajagopal, A., Dembia, C. L., DeMers, M. S., Delp, D. D., Hicks, J. L., & Delp, S. L. (2016).
  Full-body musculoskeletal model for muscle-driven simulation of human gait.
  *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*, 63(10), 2068–2079.
- Bone geometry: Delp et al. (1990), *IEEE TBME* 37(8), 757–767; Holzbaur et al. (2005),
  *Annals of Biomedical Engineering* 33(6), 829–840.

The manual renders anatomy figures from these meshes (see `../skeleton.py`, `../anat3d.py`
and `../../anatfigs.py`).
