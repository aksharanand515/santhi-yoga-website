The programme

Live, study and practise in Fort Kochi

Over 28 days you deepen your own practice while learning to teach with clarity and confidence.

The programme brings together Hatha Yoga, Vinyasa, asana, pranayama, meditation, yoga philosophy and teaching methodology under the guidance of Master Aksharanand A S, known to his students as Achu. It is designed for sincere practitioners who want a traditional foundation, whether you plan to teach or simply wish to understand yoga more fully.

What you will study

A traditional foundation

The 28 days bring together daily practice, study, learning how to guide others, and life in Fort Kochi.

  • Hatha Yoga

    The traditional practice at the heart of the school.

  • Vinyasa

    Linking movement and breath into flowing sequences.

  • Asana

    Postures, alignment and sequencing in detail.

  • Pranayama

    Breathing techniques, practised with attention and without strain.

  • Meditation

    Stillness and reflection as part of every day.

  • Yoga philosophy

    The traditional teachings behind the practice.

  • Teaching methodology

    How to guide, cue, observe and hold space for students.

  • Kerala living

    Accommodation in Fort Kochi, sattvic meals and cultural excursions.

Before you apply

Ask on WhatsApp
Who is the training for?

Sincere practitioners who want a traditional foundation, whether you plan to teach or simply wish to understand yoga more fully.

What does €899 include?

The 200-hour certification, study materials, accommodation in Fort Kochi, three sattvic vegetarian meals a day, Ayurvedic tea and cultural excursions.

How long is the course?

28 days. The 2027 batches run 1–28 January, 1–28 February, 1–28 April and 1–28 June.

Who teaches the course?

Master Aksharanand A S (Achu), who has led more than 70 international teacher training batches and teaches Hatha Yoga, Pranayama, Meditation and Vedanta Philosophy.

How do I reserve a place?

Send an enquiry by email or WhatsApp with your preferred month. We will reply with the next steps.
