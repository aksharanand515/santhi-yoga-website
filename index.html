06Before your first class

Drop-in yoga in Fort Kochi, simply answered

When the classes are, what they cost, and whether you need to book: the things people ask before they walk in for the first time.

The full fact sheet

Where can I do yoga in Fort Kochi?

At Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies in Chirattapalam, Fort Kochi: a traditional Hatha yoga school with two-hour classes every day of the week. Anywhere in Fort Kochi itself is within walking distance.

What time are the drop-in yoga classes?

Every day, with no day off: 7:30–9:30 am and 3:30–5:30 pm. Some mornings the class moves to Secret Garden, an open garden nearby, from 8:00–9:30 am, so message us on WhatsApp the evening before a morning class.

How much is a yoga class in Fort Kochi?

A two-hour drop-in class is ₹500 (a limited-time offer; normally ₹600, and ₹700 from November 2026). Practise with us for more than a week and classes are 30% off. A private session is ₹1,500, rising to ₹2,000 in November 2026. Yoga class fees in Kochi

Do I need to book a class?

No. Daily classes are walk-in, with no booking, membership or package. Mats are provided; wear clothes you can bend in and bring water.

Can complete beginners join?

Yes. Every class is open to all levels and taught in English, with easier versions of each posture and hands-on guidance. Tell the teacher if you are new or have an injury. Your first class

Is there a yoga teacher training in Fort Kochi?

Yes. The school runs a 28-day, 200-hour yoga teacher training in Fort Kochi for €899, all-inclusive of accommodation, meals and excursions, with 2027 batches in January, February, April and June. Yoga teacher training in Kerala: 2027 dates
