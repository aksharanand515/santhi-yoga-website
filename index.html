About the Teacher

Meet Master Aksharanand A S

Known to his students simply as Achu

Master Aksharanand A S teaches traditional Hatha Yoga, Pranayama, Meditation and Vedanta Philosophy at Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies in Fort Kochi.

His classes are grounded, attentive and personal — practice is approached as a way of understanding the body, the breath and the mind, rather than as exercise alone. Whether you are joining a single morning class while travelling through Kerala or spending 28 days in teacher training, you are guided with patience and care.

Teacher Training · 2027

200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Fort Kochi, Kerala

28 Days of Traditional Yoga, Study, Practice & Kerala Living

Over 28 days you will live, study and practise in Fort Kochi — deepening your own practice while learning to teach with clarity and confidence. The program brings together Hatha Yoga, Vinyasa, asana, pranayama, meditation, yoga philosophy and teaching methodology under the guidance of Master Aksharanand A S.

It is designed for sincere practitioners who want a traditional foundation, whether you plan to teach or simply wish to understand yoga more fully.

A day in training

Sample schedule

An illustrative outline of the daily rhythm. Exact timings are confirmed with enrolled students before the course begins.

  1. Morning Practice

    Traditional Hatha practice to awaken the body gently at the start of the day.

  2. Pranayama

    Guided breathing techniques, practised with attention and without strain.

  1. Breakfast

    A wholesome sattvic vegetarian breakfast.

  2. Philosophy / Theory

    Study of yoga philosophy and the traditional foundations of practice.

  3. Asana Practice

    Detailed work on postures, alignment and sequencing across Hatha and Vinyasa.

  1. Lunch

    A nourishing sattvic vegetarian lunch.

  2. Rest / Study

    Quiet time to rest, read and review your study materials.

  1. Teaching Methodology

    Learning how to guide, cue, observe and hold space for students.

  1. Evening Practice

    A slower, grounding practice as the day winds down.

  2. Meditation / Reflection

    Sitting in stillness and reflecting on the day’s learning.
