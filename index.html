Santhi means peace.

For more than fifteen years, in a quiet lane of Fort Kochi, we have taught yoga the slow way: postures held rather than hurried, corrections given by hand, rest woven through every class, and the philosophy behind the practice taught in plain words.

Master Aksharanand A S Founder & teacher

Your teacher

Traditional Hatha Yoga, taught with patience and care

Master Aksharanand A S trained in the Sivananda tradition and teaches Hatha Yoga, Pranayama, Meditation and Vedanta Philosophy at Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies in Fort Kochi.

Practice here is a way of understanding the body, the breath and the mind, not exercise alone. Whether you join a single morning class while travelling through Kerala or spend 28 days in teacher training, you are guided personally.
