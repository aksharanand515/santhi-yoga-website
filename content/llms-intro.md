# Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies (Santhi Yoga India)

> A traditional Hatha Yoga school and yoga studio in Chirattapalam, Fort Kochi, Kerala, India, led by Master Aksharanand A S (known as Achu), who trained in the Sivananda tradition. Two-hour walk-in yoga classes run every day at 7:30 am and 3:30 pm, taught in English, for all levels.

- The school is not connected to Santhi Yoga International in Vagamon, a separate organisation.
