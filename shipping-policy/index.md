# Shipping and delivery policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies sells yoga classes, private sessions and the 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training, taught in person in Fort Kochi, Kerala. Nothing physical is shipped.

## How your booking is delivered

- **Confirmation.** After you pay a deposit, we confirm your place personally on WhatsApp or by email, usually the same day and always within 2 working days.
- **Before you travel.** We send your arrival details, what to bring and how to prepare.
- **The course.** The training is taught at the school in Fort Kochi on the dates of the batch you booked. Accommodation, meals and study materials are provided there.
- **The certificate.** Students who complete the course receive the school’s certificate in person on the last day.
