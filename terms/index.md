## The teacher training and the deposit

The 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training runs for 28 days in Fort Kochi. The fee is €899, all-inclusive, as described on the [teacher training page](https://santhiyogaindia.com/teacher-training/). A deposit of €90 holds your place on the batch you choose and is part of the fee, not an extra. The balance of €809 is paid within the first three days of the course.

Places are held in the order deposits arrive. Your place is confirmed once we have received your deposit and replied to confirm it, usually the same day.

## Paying

You can pay the deposit by Wise, by bank transfer, by IMPS or NEFT within India, or by card through our payment partner where offered. Card payments are processed by that partner, and the school never sees or stores your card details. Any fee charged by your bank or payment service is yours to pay, so that the full amount reaches the school.

## Cancellations and refunds

How deposits move, and when they are refunded, is set out in our [refund and cancellation policy](https://santhiyogaindia.com/refund-policy/).

## Your health

Yoga is physical. Tell us before the course about any injury, illness, pregnancy or condition that could affect your practice, and practise within your own limits. Our teachers give hands-on adjustments; tell your teacher at any time if you would rather not be adjusted. Please take out travel and health insurance that covers your stay in India.

## The certificate

Students who complete the course, including its assessment, receive the school’s own 200-hour certificate. The school is not a Yoga Alliance Registered Yoga School, and the certificate is not a Yoga Alliance credential.

## Conduct

We ask every student to respect the teachers, fellow students and the house rules of the school and the accommodation. We may ask a student whose behaviour endangers or seriously disturbs others to leave the course.

## Changes by the school

If we have to cancel or move a batch, we will tell you as early as we can, and you can move to another batch or have your deposit refunded in full. We are not responsible for flights, visas or other travel costs, so we recommend booking flexible travel.
