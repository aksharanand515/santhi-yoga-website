Santhi Yoga in Fort Kochi: your questions answered

The facts about Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies in one place: when classes run, what they cost, who teaches, what happens in a class, and how to join.

By Master Aksharanand A S, founder of Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies Fort Kochi, Kerala Updated 1 November 2026 5 min read

In short

Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies is a traditional Hatha Yoga school and yoga studio in Chirattapalam, Fort Kochi, Kerala, led by Master Aksharanand A S. Two-hour walk-in classes run every day at 7:30 am and 3:30 pm, in English, for all levels, at ₹700 a class. No booking is needed.

People find us in different ways: on Google Maps, on Tripadvisor, through a guesthouse owner, or by asking an AI assistant where to practise yoga in Fort Kochi. Wherever you started, this page gathers the questions we are asked most often, with answers that are accurate and up to date.

Daily classes

When are the classes?

Every day, with no day off: mornings 7:30–9:30 am and afternoons 3:30–5:30 pm. Some mornings the class moves to Secret Garden, an open garden nearby, from 8:00–9:30 am, so send a message the evening before a morning class. The afternoon class is always at the school.

What happens in a class?

Every class follows the same traditional order. It opens with savasana, lying down to settle the body and mind and create the atmosphere for practice. Then comes pranayama, breathing practice; then Surya Namaskar, the Sun Salutation; then asanas for all levels; and finally a deep, long relaxation.

Do I need experience?

No. Beginners and experienced students practise in the same room, with easier and stronger versions of each posture and hands-on guidance. Tell the teacher if you are new or have an injury. Our beginner’s guide explains what to expect.

Do I need to book or bring anything?

No booking is needed, and mats are provided. Wear clothes you can bend in, and bring water and a light layer for the final relaxation.

Three things worth being clear about

We are not Santhi Yoga International in Vagamon

A similarly named school, Santhi Yoga International, operates in Vagamon, elsewhere in Kerala. It is a separate organisation with no connection to us. Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies is in Fort Kochi, and its phone number is +91 7907714144.

We are not a Yoga Alliance Registered Yoga School

Graduates of our 200-hour training receive the school’s own certificate and cannot register as RYT 200 with Yoga Alliance. We explain what that means in how to choose a teacher training.

We are independent of the Sivananda organisation

Our teacher trained in the Sivananda tradition, and that training shapes the classes, but the school is not an official Sivananda centre. See Sivananda yoga in Kochi.

Common questions

Where is Santhi Yoga in Fort Kochi?

Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies is at House No 1/1790, Chirattapalam Road, Fort Kochi, Kochi, Kerala 682001. Some mornings the class is held at Secret Garden, an open garden nearby, so message +91 7907714144 the evening before a morning class.

What time are classes at Santhi Yoga?

Every day, with no day off: 7:30–9:30 am and 3:30–5:30 pm. On mornings when the class moves to Secret Garden it runs 8:00–9:30 am.

How much does a class at Santhi Yoga cost?

A two-hour drop-in class is ₹700, with 30% off for students who practise for more than a week. A private session is ₹2,000.

Is Santhi Yoga suitable for beginners?

Yes. Classes are for all levels, taught in English with hands-on guidance, and mats are provided. Tell the teacher when you arrive if you are new or have an injury.

Is Santhi Yoga in Fort Kochi the same as Santhi Yoga International?

No. Santhi Yoga International in Vagamon is a separate, unconnected organisation. Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies is in Fort Kochi.

Is Santhi Yoga’s teacher training Yoga Alliance certified?

No. The school is not a Yoga Alliance Registered Yoga School. Graduates of the 28-day, 200-hour training receive the school’s own certificate.
