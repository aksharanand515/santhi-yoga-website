## Daily classes

### When are the classes?

Every day, with no day off: mornings 7:30–9:30 am and afternoons 3:30–5:30 pm. Some mornings the class moves to Secret Garden, an open garden nearby, from 8:00–9:30 am, so send a message the evening before a morning class. The afternoon class is always at the school.

### What happens in a class?

Every class follows the same traditional order. It opens with savasana, lying down to settle the body and mind and create the atmosphere for practice. Then comes pranayama, breathing practice; then [Surya Namaskar](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/surya-namaskar-steps/), the Sun Salutation; then asanas for all levels; and finally a deep, long relaxation.

### Do I need experience?

No. Beginners and experienced students practise in the same room, with easier and stronger versions of each posture and hands-on guidance. Tell the teacher if you are new or have an injury. Our [beginner’s guide](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/first-yoga-class-beginners-guide/) explains what to expect.

### Do I need to book or bring anything?

No booking is needed, and mats are provided. Wear clothes you can bend in, and bring water and a light layer for the final relaxation.

## Three things worth being clear about

### We are not Santhi Yoga International in Vagamon

A similarly named school, Santhi Yoga International, operates in Vagamon, elsewhere in Kerala. It is a separate organisation with no connection to us. Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies is in Fort Kochi, and its phone number is +91 7907714144.

### We are not a Yoga Alliance Registered Yoga School

Graduates of our 200-hour training receive the school’s own certificate and cannot register as RYT 200 with Yoga Alliance. We explain what that means in [how to choose a teacher training](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/how-to-choose-yoga-teacher-training/).

### We are independent of the Sivananda organisation

Our teacher trained in the Sivananda tradition, and that training shapes the classes, but the school is not an official Sivananda centre. See [Sivananda yoga in Kochi](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/sivananda-yoga-kochi/).
