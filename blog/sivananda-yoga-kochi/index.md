In short

**Sivananda yoga is a classical Hatha tradition built on five points: proper exercise, breathing, relaxation, diet, and positive thinking with meditation.** Its classes follow a steady sequence of breathing, sun salutations and twelve basic postures with rest between them. The official Sivananda centres in Kerala are in Trivandrum and at Neyyar Dam; in Kochi, you practise with teachers trained in the tradition.

Every week, someone searches for "Sivananda yoga Ernakulam" or "Sivananda yoga Kochi" and finds very little. That is not surprising, because there is no official Sivananda centre in the city. ## Where Sivananda yoga comes from

The tradition takes its name from **Swami Sivananda of Rishikesh**, a physician who became a monk and one of the most influential yoga teachers of the twentieth century. His teaching joined the physical practice of Hatha Yoga with philosophy, devotion, selfless service and meditation.

His disciple **Swami Vishnudevananda** founded the Sivananda Yoga Vedanta Centres and shaped that teaching into a form that ordinary students could practise every day: five simple points, and a class built around twelve basic postures. That structure is what most people mean today when they say "Sivananda yoga".

## The five points of yoga

| Point | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Proper exercise** — asana | Postures that keep the spine supple and the body strong, practised slowly and held |
| **2. Proper breathing** — pranayama | Full, conscious breathing, and specific breathing practices at the start of class |
| **3. Proper relaxation** — savasana | Deliberate rest between postures and at the end, so the body absorbs the practice |
| **4. Proper diet** — vegetarian | Simple, fresh, vegetarian food that supports a calm, clear mind |
| **5. Positive thinking and meditation** — Vedanta and raja yoga | Training the mind: study, reflection and regular meditation |

Only the first three happen on the mat. The point of the tradition is that yoga does not stop when the class ends. The fifth point draws on [Vedanta](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/what-is-vedanta/), and the wider path is set out in [the eight limbs of yoga](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/eight-limbs-of-yoga/).

## The twelve basic postures

At the heart of a Sivananda class is a sequence of twelve postures, practised in a set order so that every part of the body is moved and then counterbalanced. Beginners work towards them gradually, with easier versions.

1. **Headstand** — Sirsasana
2. **Shoulderstand** — Sarvangasana
3. **Plough** — Halasana
4. **Fish** — Matsyasana
5. **Seated forward bend** — Paschimottanasana
6. **Cobra** — Bhujangasana
7. **Locust** — Salabhasana
8. **Bow** — Dhanurasana
9. **Half spinal twist** — Ardha Matsyendrasana
10. **Crow** — Kakasana
11. **Standing forward bend** — Pada Hastasana
12. **Triangle** — Trikonasana

## What a class in the tradition involves

A class shaped by Sivananda teaching is recognisable by its rhythm rather than its difficulty:

- **Settling and relaxation**, often with a short chant.
- **Breathing practices**, such as kapalabhati and alternate nostril breathing.
- **Sun salutations** to warm the body — we have written out the [Sivananda Sun Salutation step by step](https://santhiyogaindia.com/blog/surya-namaskar-steps/).
- **The postures**, held rather than flowed through, with short rests between.
- **A long final relaxation.**

Compared with a Vinyasa class, it is slower, quieter and more systematic. That is precisely why it suits beginners, and why many experienced practitioners come back to it.

## Common questions

### Is there a Sivananda yoga centre in Kochi or Ernakulam?

Not an official one that we are aware of. The official Sivananda Yoga Vedanta centres in Kerala are the city centre in Trivandrum and the ashram at Neyyar Dam. In Kochi you can practise with independent teachers trained in the tradition.

### What are the five points of Sivananda yoga?

Proper exercise (asana), proper breathing (pranayama), proper relaxation (savasana), proper diet (vegetarian), and positive thinking and meditation (Vedanta and raja yoga), as set out by Swami Vishnudevananda.

### What are the 12 basic postures in Sivananda yoga?

Headstand, shoulderstand, plough, fish, seated forward bend, cobra, locust, bow, half spinal twist, crow, standing forward bend and triangle, practised in that order with rest between them.

### Is Sivananda yoga good for beginners?

Yes. Its slow, systematic pace, the rest between postures and the emphasis on breathing make it one of the most beginner-friendly traditions. Harder postures such as headstand are approached gradually, with easier versions first.

### How is Sivananda yoga different from Vinyasa?

Sivananda classes hold postures and rest between them in a largely fixed sequence. Vinyasa links postures into continuous flowing movement that changes from class to class, so it feels faster and more like a workout.
