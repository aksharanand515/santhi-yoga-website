# Kochi airport to Fort Kochi, *and how to get here*

Cochin International Airport is about 45 kilometres north of Fort Kochi, and the journey takes around an hour and a half. These are the ways people actually do it.

By **Master Aksharanand A S**, founder of Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies Fort Kochi, Kerala Updated 23 September 2026 5 min read

In short

**A pre-paid or app taxi from Cochin International Airport to Fort Kochi takes about an hour and a half and is what most visitors take.** The cheaper route is an airport bus or train to Ernakulam and then the ferry across, which is slower, far more interesting, and only runs during daylight.

Cochin International Airport, code COK, sits at Nedumbassery, north-east of the city. Fort Kochi is on the coast at the far south-western end — a different world from the airport, and a longer drive than the map suggests, because you cross the whole of Ernakulam to get there.

## The short answer

- **Simplest:** pre-paid taxi from the counter in arrivals, or an app cab. Around 90 minutes.
- **Cheapest:** bus or train to Ernakulam, then the ferry to Fort Kochi. Two to three hours, and a much better story.
- **Late at night:** taxi, arranged in advance if you can.

## By taxi

Inside arrivals there is a pre-paid taxi counter where you pay a fixed fare at the desk and hand the slip to the driver. This avoids negotiating at the kerb, and is the easy option after a long flight. Ride-hailing apps also work at the airport, and quote the fare before you book, though the pickup point is not always obvious late at night.

The drive runs about 45 kilometres. Allow ninety minutes, and rather more in the evening rush or heavy monsoon rain. Any hotel or guesthouse in Fort Kochi will also arrange a pickup for you if you send them your flight number — worth doing for a first visit.

## By bus, train and ferry

The frugal route has three legs, and Indian travellers use it constantly:

1. **Airport to Aluva.** Regular buses run between the airport and Aluva, a short distance away.
2. **Aluva to Ernakulam.** The Kochi Metro runs from Aluva into the city, and trains run to Ernakulam stations too. Get off in the city centre, near the boat jetty.
3. **Ernakulam to Fort Kochi.** The public ferry from Ernakulam boat jetty crosses to Fort Kochi in about twenty minutes for a few rupees.

There are also direct buses from the airport towards the city. Timings change, so check locally on the day rather than trusting any schedule you read online — including this one.

## The ferry

The Ernakulam to Fort Kochi ferry is the nicest arrival in the city: twenty minutes across the harbour, past the container ships, with the old town appearing slowly. It runs frequently through the day and stops in the evening, so it is a daytime option only. Travelling light makes it much easier — there are steps and gangways, and nobody enjoys carrying a large suitcase onto a boat.

## If you land late

Many international flights arrive in the small hours. Take a taxi; the ferry will not be running and the buses thin out. If your guesthouse is expecting you, message them your arrival time so somebody is awake to let you in — Fort Kochi goes to bed early.

## Common questions

**How far is Fort Kochi from Cochin International Airport?**

About 45 kilometres. The drive normally takes around an hour and a half, longer in evening traffic or heavy monsoon rain.

**What is the cheapest way from Kochi airport to Fort Kochi?**

Bus or metro to Ernakulam, then the public ferry across to Fort Kochi, which takes about twenty minutes and costs a few rupees. Allow two to three hours in total, and note that the ferry only runs during the day.

**Is there a ferry to Fort Kochi?**

Yes. Public ferries cross regularly from Ernakulam boat jetty to Fort Kochi in around twenty minutes during daylight hours. It is the most pleasant way to arrive if you are travelling light.

**How do I get to Fort Kochi late at night?**

Take a taxi — the pre-paid counter in arrivals or a ride-hailing app. Ferries do not run at night and buses are infrequent. Tell your guesthouse your arrival time so someone is expecting you.
