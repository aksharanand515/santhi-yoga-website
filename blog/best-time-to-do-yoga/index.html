Morning or evening yoga: what is the best time to practise?

The traditional answer, what actually changes between morning and evening practice, how Kerala’s heat and humidity shift the balance, and how to choose between a morning and an afternoon class.

By Master Aksharanand A S, founder of Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies Fort Kochi, Kerala Updated 1 November 2026 4 min read

In short

Traditionally, yoga is practised in the early morning on an empty stomach, when the mind is quiet. Evening practice suits many bodies better, because muscles are warmer and more flexible. The best time is the one you can keep up, at least two to three hours after a meal. In Kerala, avoid the midday heat.

"Should I come in the morning or the afternoon?" is one of the first questions people ask when they message us, and it is a good one, because the two classes feel quite different. The morning is cooler and quieter. The afternoon finds the body warmer and looser.

The broader question — what is the best time of day to do yoga at all — has a traditional answer and a practical one, and they do not always agree. Our two classes run at 7:30 am and 3:30 pm; the details are in our guide to yoga in Fort Kochi.

The traditional answer: early morning

Yoga texts and teachers have long favoured the early morning, and especially brahma muhurta, the period before sunrise, often described as around an hour and a half before dawn. The reasoning is practical as much as spiritual: the stomach is empty, the house is quiet, the mind has not yet filled with the day, and practice sets the tone for everything after.

You do not need to rise at 4:30 am to benefit from this. Practising before breakfast, before the phone, and before the day's demands is the heart of it.

Neither column wins. If you are stiff and want to feel your range, the afternoon may feel better. If you want calm and discipline, the morning usually delivers them more reliably.

What matters more than the clock

  • Consistency. The same time, several days a week, beats the "perfect" time done occasionally. Choose the slot your life actually allows.
  • The meal gap. About three hours after a full meal, two after something light. This matters more than the hour of the day.
  • Matching the practice to the time. More energising work — sun salutations, standing postures — suits the morning. Slower forward bends, breathing and relaxation suit the evening.
  • Not rushing. A calm half hour is worth more than a hurried hour squeezed between commitments.

Practising in Kerala’s climate

Kochi's heat changes the calculation. Humidity averages from around 61% in January to 83% in July, and March and April are the hottest months. A few things make practice here more comfortable:

  • Avoid the middle of the day. Early morning and late afternoon are the times to practise; midday is for shade and lunch.
  • Drink through the day, not just before class.
  • Wear breathable clothes — our guide to what to wear to a yoga class in India has the details.
  • Stop if you feel dizzy or nauseous, sit down, and tell the teacher.
  • In the monsoon, humidity is at its highest but the air is often cooler. Many people find practice deeply settling in the rains.

If you are staying for several days, try both. Many students find they have a strong preference by the third class — and it is often not the one they expected. For the rest of your day, our local’s guide to Fort Kochi is arranged by time of day for exactly this reason.

If you can only practise at night

Practise anyway. Keep it gentle: slow stretches, forward bends, calm breathing such as alternate nostril breathing, and a long relaxation. Leave vigorous or strongly energising practices for earlier in the day, and give yourself a little time between practice and sleep. Chandra Namaskar, the moon salutation, is built for exactly this slot.

Common questions

Is it better to do yoga in the morning or the evening?

Traditionally, early morning is preferred because the stomach is empty and the mind is quiet. Evening suits many people because the body is warmer and more flexible. The best time is the one you can keep up regularly.

How long after eating should I do yoga?

About three hours after a full meal, or two hours after something light such as fruit or a small breakfast. Practising on a full stomach makes twists and forward bends uncomfortable.

Is it OK to do yoga at night?

Yes. Keep evening practice gentle — slow stretches, calm breathing and relaxation — and save vigorous or energising practices for earlier in the day.

What is brahma muhurta?

The period before sunrise, often described as around an hour and a half before dawn, traditionally considered the best time for yoga and meditation because the mind is naturally quiet.

What time are yoga classes in Fort Kochi?

At Santhi Yoga, the morning class runs 7:30–9:30 am at the school, or 8:00–9:30 am on mornings when it moves to Secret Garden, and the afternoon class runs 3:30–5:30 pm at the school. Message us the evening before a morning class.

Can I do yoga in the heat in Kerala?

Yes, with care. Avoid midday, drink water through the day, wear breathable clothes, and stop and sit down if you feel dizzy or nauseous. Early morning and late afternoon are the most comfortable times.
