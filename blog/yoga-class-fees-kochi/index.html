Yoga class fees in Kochi: what you should pay

Monthly group classes, private tutors, at-home sessions, tourist experiences and drop-in classes, compared honestly — including where ours sits and when it is not the best value for you.

By Master Aksharanand A S, founder of Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies Fort Kochi, Kerala Updated 17 September 2026 4 min read

In short

In Kochi, regular group yoga classes average roughly ₹400–500 a month, private tutors average around ₹450–650 an hour, and sessions booked through tourist platforms usually start near ₹3,000. Drop-in classes aimed at visitors sit in between. Price tracks class length, group size, teacher experience, and who the class is designed for.

"Yoga classes Kochi fees" is one of the most common things people search before they choose a class, and the answers online are either vague or a sales pitch. This page puts the real ranges side by side, using figures published on listing sites in September 2026, and explains why they differ so much.

It includes our own price, and it is honest about who we are not the cheapest option for. If you are visiting and only want class times, our guide to yoga in Fort Kochi has them.

Why the same hour of yoga costs ₹20 or ₹2,000

Five things drive the price, and only some of them are about quality.

  • Group size. A monthly class for thirty people can charge very little per person. A class of eight cannot.
  • Class length. A two-hour traditional class and a 45-minute gym session are not the same product.
  • The teacher. Years of teaching, the depth of their training, and whether they correct you individually.
  • Who the class is for. Classes designed for visitors in tourist areas cost more than neighbourhood classes for residents.
  • Commission. Booking platforms take a share, and it is built into what you pay.

Common questions

How much is a yoga class in Kochi?

Monthly group classes average roughly ₹400–500 a month on listing sites, drop-in classes aimed at visitors vary by school, and sessions booked through tourist platforms often start near ₹3,000. Our two-hour drop-in class in Fort Kochi is ₹600, rising to ₹700 in November 2026.

How much does a private yoga teacher cost in Kochi?

Private yoga tutors listed on Superprof average about ₹641 an hour in Kochi and about ₹459 an hour in Ernakulam, starting from around ₹399. At-home sessions appear on some listings at around ₹1,000. Experienced teachers usually charge more.

Are trial yoga classes free in Kochi?

Often. Many private tutors on listing sites offer a free first lesson, and many centres let you try a class before paying a monthly fee. Ask before you go.

Why are yoga classes for tourists more expensive?

They are usually longer or packaged as an experience, taught in tourist areas where costs are higher, and often sold through booking platforms that take a commission. A drop-in class booked directly with a school is usually cheaper.
