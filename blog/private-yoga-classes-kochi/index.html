Private yoga classes in Kochi: who they help, and what to expect

When one-to-one teaching is worth the extra cost, what a good first session looks like, what private yoga costs in Kochi, and how to tell a teacher from someone with a certificate.

By Master Aksharanand A S, founder of Santhi School of Yoga & Vedanta Studies Fort Kochi, Kerala Updated 17 September 2026 4 min read

In short

A private yoga class is worth it if you have an injury or health condition, feel self-conscious in groups, want a home practice built for you, or need to work around your schedule. Private tutors in Kochi average roughly ₹450–650 an hour on listing sites. A good first session begins with questions, not postures.

Most people do not need private yoga. A good group class, with a teacher who knows your name and your knee, covers a great deal. But for some people, one-to-one teaching is not a luxury — it is the difference between starting safely and not starting at all.

This guide explains who those people are, what a first private session should actually look like, and what it costs in Kochi. If you would rather begin in a group, our guide to yoga in Fort Kochi has our daily class times.

Who private sessions help most

  • People with injuries or health conditions — back or neck problems, knee or hip issues, high blood pressure — once a doctor has said exercise is appropriate. A teacher can work around the problem rather than asking you to modify a class designed for everyone else. See also yoga for back pain.
  • Complete beginners who feel self-conscious in a room of strangers. A few private sessions can make a group class feel easy afterwards.
  • Older students starting for the first time, or returning after a long gap.
  • People with a specific goal: a safe headstand, a pranayama practice, a routine for a stiff back after desk work, a sequence for travel.
  • Anyone who wants a home practice they can actually keep, built for their body and the time they really have.
  • Pregnancy, with your doctor's approval and a teacher experienced in prenatal yoga. Ask directly about that experience.

Some teachers also offer semi-private sessions for two or three people, such as couples, friends or family members. Ask if that suits you.

What happens in a first private session

A good first session follows a shape. Be wary of one that starts with sun salutations the moment you walk in.

  1. Conversation. Your health, injuries, previous exercise and yoga, what you want, and how much time you have to practise.
  2. Observation. How you stand, breathe and move through a few simple movements.
  3. Practice built around you. Postures and breathing chosen for what the teacher has just seen, with clear explanation of why.
  4. Something to take home. A short sequence you can remember and do alone.
  5. A plan. How often to practise, and whether and when to meet again.

This is the heart of how we teach private sessions: time to look closely at how you move and breathe, and to build a practice you can continue on your own.

What private yoga costs in Kochi

On listing sites in September 2026, private yoga tutors averaged about ₹641 an hour in Kochi and about ₹459 an hour in Ernakulam, starting from around ₹399 (Superprof). How to choose a private teacher

  • Training and lineage, not just a certificate. Where did they train, in what tradition, and for how long?
  • Years of teaching real people, including people with limitations like yours.
  • They ask about your health before anything else.
  • They explain why they are giving you each posture.
  • They make themselves less necessary over time, by giving you a practice you can do alone.

How much does a private yoga class cost in Kochi?

How many private yoga sessions do I need?

It depends on your goal. Often a few sessions are enough to learn a safe home practice, followed by occasional check-ins. For an injury or health condition, regular sessions over several weeks are more useful.

Is private yoga good for back pain?

It can be, once a doctor has confirmed that exercise is appropriate. A private teacher can design a gentle practice around your back rather than asking you to modify a group class. Stop anything that causes sharp pain.

Can a beginner start with private yoga classes?

Yes, and it is one of the best ways to begin, especially if you feel self-conscious or have health concerns. A few private sessions make joining a group class much easier afterwards.

Can a yoga teacher come to my home in Kochi?

Many private tutors in Kochi offer home visits, often at a higher rate to cover travel. Ask us where and how our private sessions can take place.
